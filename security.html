    Security

    Last updated: November 14, 2026

    You are trusting us with your pipeline, your buyers and your calls. Here is exactly how we protect it, in plain English - including an honest account of what we are not yet certified for. If your security team needs to go deeper than this page, we will get on a call and walk them through the architecture.

    1. Your data is isolated at the database

    Every account's data sits behind row-level security enforced inside the database itself (Supabase, managed PostgreSQL), not just in the application. Two rules do the work: an owner only ever sees their own rows, and a manager can read only the rows that belong to their own reps. Another company never sees your data, and another rep on your own team only sees what they are entitled to. Because it is enforced one layer down - at the database, not in app code - the isolation holds even if application code has a bug.

    2. Encryption

    All traffic between you and DealMentor is encrypted in transit with TLS. Your data is encrypted at rest by our database provider.

    3. Hosting and infrastructure

    DealMentor runs on Supabase (managed PostgreSQL), which is SOC 2 Type II certified and provides our database, authentication and storage. We operate from Israel; Supabase and our AI provider process data in the United States and other regions.

    4. How AI handles your data

    Generation runs through the Anthropic Claude API. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, your inputs are not used to train public models - yours or theirs. Every AI output carries an anti-hallucination rule and a CRO sign-off: the system is built to reason only from what is actually in your account, not to invent facts. Your deal data is never sold and never shared with advertisers.

    5. Authentication

    Sign-in is email and password today, with secure password reset. Single sign-on (SSO / SAML) is on the roadmap for larger teams. If it is a requirement for you, tell us on a call and we will tell you straight where it stands.

    6. Payments

    Billing runs through Paddle, our merchant of record. Payment webhooks are signature-verified and fail closed, so a forged billing event cannot change your account. We never store full card numbers.

    7. Integrations and getting your data out

    DealMentor runs standalone in the browser - there is no integration to wire up and no IT project to start. Two-way sync with email, calendar and dialers is on the roadmap, not shipped today. Your data is always yours: import your pipeline from a CSV, and export your deals and contacts back to CSV in one click, any time. No lock-in, month to month.

    8. Certifications - the honest version

    We will not claim a badge we have not earned. Our infrastructure provider (Supabase) is SOC 2 Type II certified, but DealMentor itself is not yet independently SOC 2, ISO or HIPAA certified. Business customers who need a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) or a current sub-processor list can request one at support@thedealmentor.ai. For anything deeper, the honest answer is the best one: we get on a call and walk your security team through the architecture, the data flows and the controls, and we answer whatever your questionnaire asks.

    9. Reporting a vulnerability

    Found something? Email support@thedealmentor.ai with "Security" in the subject and the details, and we will respond quickly. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will work with you.
