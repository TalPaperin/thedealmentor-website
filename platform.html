How it works

Registration to field-ready in three steps.

No integrations. No technical setup. No long onboarding. Most users have a complete sales program within 15 minutes of signing up.

Step 01

Describe your business (or paste the client brief)

Founders and teams: tell the platform about your product, your ideal buyer, your competitors, and your top objections - a short guided setup, or hand over a deck and it drafts the rest. BPOs and centers: paste the client's campaign brief into a New Campaign. Upload your decks, pricing, case studies, or any campaign materials to the Knowledge Base. The more specific the input, the sharper every output - permanently.

Step 02

Generate your program (or your Campaign Pack)

One click. Founders / teams: your full Sales Program - playbook, ICP scripts, marketing/demand-gen, and the supporting documents for your mode - generates in parallel and streams in within 2-3 minutes. BPOs: a Campaign Pack (cheat-sheet + rebuttal library + certification) generates in about a minute, ready to send your agents into.

Step 03

Use it in real deals (or on the dialer)

Founders / teams: open the platform before every call. Use it when an objection lands. Run it when a deal stalls. BPOs: certify the agents on the campaign, then run Practice-a-Call, then send them to the dialer with the cheat-sheet and rebuttals in the next browser tab. The system is built for live situations - not just prep or post-deal review.
