Common Questions

Direct answers. No fluff.

What is DealMentor AI?
DealMentor is a virtual CRO platform built by KSW Solutions. It gives founders and sales teams the tools, structure, and judgment of a full sales leadership team , including a CRO, VP Sales, SDR Manager, and AE Coach , available 24/7. It's powered by 50+ proven B2B sales methodologies and grounded in your specific business context. Plans start at $149/month.
How is DealMentor different from ChatGPT or other AI tools?
DealMentor is not a general-purpose AI. It doesn't reset between sessions and it's not a prompt interface. Every output is built from your specific product, pricing, ICP, and competitive landscape , and it builds context over time as you use it. The difference is direction vs. content: other tools give you things to adapt, DealMentor tells you what to do.
How is this different from Gong or Salesloft?
Gong and Salesloft are recording and sequencing tools , they analyze what happened after the fact. DealMentor is a pre-deal and in-deal system: it tells you what to say before the call, how to respond when an objection lands, and what to do when a deal stalls. It's direction, not data.
Does every plan really include access to a real CRO?
Yes. Every paying plan includes structured access to Tal Paperin , the fractional CRO and founder of KSW Solutions, with 20+ years of B2B sales experience across the US, Israel, Asia, and Europe. The format (sessions + async support) scales with your plan level. It's built into the product, not a separate engagement or add-on.
What does setup actually look like?
Registration takes two minutes. The intake form , where you describe your product, buyer, and competitive landscape , takes about 5 minutes to complete. From there, your full sales program generates in 2–3 minutes. No integrations, no technical setup, no IT involvement. Most users are field-ready within 15 minutes of signing up.
Is this for founders who sell alone, or do I need a team?
Both. Founder Mode is built for founders selling themselves , pre-SDR, pre-team , and gives you a complete personal sales program. Team Mode is built for sales managers who need to align reps, standardize execution, and certify their team on the company playbook. You choose your mode at registration.
What happens after the free trial?
At the end of your trial you choose a plan to continue. Everything you built during the trial , your playbook, scripts, outputs, and uploaded documents , stays in your account. If you don't subscribe, your account pauses but your data isn't deleted. No credit card is required to start the trial.
