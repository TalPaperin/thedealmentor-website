The problem

Your CRM is a filing cabinet.

Salesforce and HubSpot are a data-entry tax with no payoff. Reps do not update them, so the pipeline lies, so the manager coaches blind. A CRM that gives nothing back gets nothing entered.

Fire 02

Reps wing the email. New hires ramp for 60 to 90 days. Your best rep's instincts never reach the team. You need the actual words, per deal, per buyer, per objection, in the moment, not a course.

A full-time CRO runs $300K to $500K a year. Fractional is $6,000 to $20,000 a month. Your VP is buried, your manager can't ride every call, and the deal in front of your rep gets no senior judgment at all.
