The problem

Twenty years across four continents. Hundreds of teams. Thousands of deals. Every revenue org, from a solo founder to a 500-agent contact center, hits the same three walls. They stay stuck there until something changes.

Wall 01

Nobody has the right words.

Reps wing the email. Agents fumble the objection. Founders freeze on the call. Theory doesn't help. Frameworks don't help. You need the actual words, per buyer, per objection, per campaign.

Wall 02

A full-time CRO costs $300K to $500K a year. Fractional runs $6,000 to $20,000 a month. Sales managers have one but never see them on a deal. BPO ops directors have an Ops VP, not a revenue leader who owns the script. Founders can't afford one at all. Same hole, three different shapes.

Your stack doesn't tell anyone what to do.

Gong records what already happened. Salesloft sequences the email. HubSpot stores the deal. Cogito and Balto listen to live calls but never wrote the script. None of them tell the seller or the agent what to do, and all of them assume the playbook already exists.
