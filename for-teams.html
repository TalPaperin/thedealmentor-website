Where teams hit the wall

Your top three reps drive 60%. The rest never catch up.

Every 5-50 rep team hits the same four bottlenecks. They are not headcount problems. They are can't-be-everywhere-at-once problems.

Problem 01

New rep ramp takes 60-90 days. Quota windows close.

Every new hire is paying onboarding cost without delivering revenue. Half wash out before they reach productive output. Your training program scales linearly with headcount and steals manager time.

Problem 02

Your manager can't ride every call.

Manager-to-rep ratio is 1:8 or worse. They join one or two calls per rep per week. The rest of the calls happen unobserved, uncoached, with each rep doing their own version of the script.

Problem 03

Your best rep's instincts don't reach the team.

The objection handler your top closer invented Monday never makes it into Wednesday's training. QA spots the divergence after the fact. Customer experience varies by who picks up the phone.

Problem 04

You can't afford a full-time CRO. Your VP is overwhelmed.

A full-time CRO is $300K-500K a year. Your VP of Sales is firefighting deals and managing reps. Nobody is doing the structural work: program design, segmentation, comp plans, certification cadence.
