Salesforce is the world's system of record. It remembers every field you make your reps fill in. What it never does is tell the rep what to do next. DealMentor is a real CRM your team runs pipeline in, with a CRO reading every deal and handing back the move, the script, and the research. Honest read of both.

The 30-second verdict

Salesforce stores the deal. DealMentor coaches the rep.

Salesforce is a configurable database of record built for large RevOps organisations with admins, integrations, and a multi-year roadmap. It is superb at holding data and terrible at telling a rep what to do with it. DealMentor is a CRM with a CRO inside: the same pipeline, contacts, activity and tasks, but every deal is read by an AI CRO that hands back the next move, and it ships with a full suite of AI tools and a live human CRO.

Bottom line up front

Salesforce Sales Cloud Enterprise lists at about $165 per user per month (roughly $2,000 a user a year), and the real cost is the admin, the integrations, and the months of configuration on top. It assumes you bring the playbook, the process, and the people to run it. DealMentor runs from $399/month for a whole team (up to 15 reps on Growth for about $12,000 a year, all in), sets up in fifteen minutes, and includes the coaching Salesforce never gave you. If you are a 200-rep enterprise with a RevOps team and hundreds of integrations, keep Salesforce. If you are a founder or a team of up to 15 reps (Enterprise beyond) who want the CRM to actually coach, that is DealMentor - and you can import your Salesforce pipeline from a CSV.

Who each is for

The fit, in plain English.

Pick Salesforce if…

  • You run a large enterprise with a dedicated RevOps or Salesforce admin team.
  • You need deep, bespoke customization - custom objects, flows, and territory logic.
  • You depend on hundreds of AppExchange integrations wired into your stack.
  • You have complex, multi-team approval and forecasting processes to model.
  • You have the budget and the quarters to configure and maintain it.

Pick DealMentor if…

  • You are a founder or a team of up to 15 reps and want a CRM that works on day one, not after a rollout.
  • You want the CRM to tell the rep the next move, not just store the deal.
  • You want prospect research, outreach, certification and simulation in the same product.
  • You want a live fractional CRO included, not a $165/seat database.
  • You would rather replace a $140-290K stack than assemble and admin one.
