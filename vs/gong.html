The 30-second verdict

Gong tells you what happened. DealMentor tells you what to do.

Gong records every call and mines it for signal - a genuinely strong tool once you are past 30 reps with a defined motion and the call volume to feed it. But it is a rear-view mirror: it grades the call after it is over. DealMentor works the other end of the call. It builds the playbook, certifies and simulates before the rep dials, copilots in the moment, and reads every deal in the CRM.

Bottom line up front

Gong doesn't publish pricing; industry reporting puts it around $1,400 to $1,600 per user per year plus a platform fee of roughly $5,000 to $10,000 and a separate onboarding fee, on annual contracts. It shines once you have call volume and a motion to analyze. DealMentor is from $399/month for the whole team and works on day one from zero. If you are 30-plus reps with a defined motion and want deep call analytics, Gong is excellent - and you can add it later. If you still need to build the motion, certify the reps, and coach before the call, that is DealMentor.

Who each is for

The fit, in plain English.

Pick Gong if…

  • You have 30-plus reps and a defined sales motion already running.
  • Your bottleneck is visibility into what's said on calls, at scale.
  • You have the call volume and history for AI analysis to be meaningful.
  • You want deal and forecast intelligence mined from conversations.
  • You have an enterprise budget for annual platform contracts.

Pick DealMentor if…

  • You need to build the playbook and motion in the first place.
  • You want to coach before the call, not just review it after.
  • You want certification and AI simulation so reps ramp before they dial.
  • You want a CRM that reads every deal and a copilot in the moment.
  • You want a live CRO included, and to work from day one with no call history.
