Where founders hit the wall

You're not stuck. You're outnumbered.

Every solo founder hits the same four problems. They are not founder problems. They are no-CRO-in-the-room problems.

Problem 01

You don't know what to say.

In the call, the email, the follow-up. Theory doesn't help. Frameworks don't help. You need the actual words for your buyer, your product, the specific objection in front of you - not a generic template.

Problem 02

You can't afford a CRO.

A full-time CRO is $300K-500K a year. A fractional one is $6K-20K a month. You can't justify either. So you wing it - and your top three competitors all hired one.

Problem 03

You spend too much time on the work that isn't selling.

Writing the email. Researching the account. Drafting the proposal. Logging the deal. Building the spreadsheet. Most of your day is spent on the work around selling, not the selling itself.

Problem 04

At 11pm with a hard call tomorrow, no one to ask.

Your accountant can't help. Your co-founder is technical. Your spouse is asleep. You need someone with 20 years of revenue experience to read the deal and tell you the next move - on demand, at any hour.
