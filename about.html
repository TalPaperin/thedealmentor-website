Where this came from

I started as an SDR.

SDR. Then AE. Then Team Leader. Then VP Global Sales and Business Development , a role I held at both multinational corporations and early-stage startups across four continents. Twenty years. Hundreds of sales teams. Thousands of deals.

And throughout all of it, I kept seeing the same exact problem. Salespeople , and founders trying to sell themselves , hitting the same wall. They don't know who to sell to. They don't know how to sell. They don't know what to say, practically, in the actual call or email or follow-up. Not theory. Not frameworks. The actual words.

The big companies can solve this. They hire a CRO at $300K-$500K a year. They bring in VP Sales consultants. They pay for Sales Enablement teams. They have the budget to build the playbook, train the reps, and iterate until it works.

But what about the founder closing deals themselves? The startup with 3 reps who can't afford a full-time CRO? The company that just hired their first SDR team and has no idea how to build a program? That's who we built this for.
