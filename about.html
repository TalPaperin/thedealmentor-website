From the founder

I started as an SDR.

SDR. Then AE. Then Team Leader. Then VP Global Sales and Business Development - a role I held at both multinational corporations and early-stage startups across four continents. Twenty years. Hundreds of sales teams. Thousands of deals.

Throughout all of it, I kept seeing the same problem at three very different scales. The solo founder closing deals herself with no playbook. The sales manager riding shotgun on 1 of every 8 of his reps' calls and guessing about the rest. The BPO ops director launching a new client campaign Monday with no audit-ready certification by Friday. Different headcounts, different KPIs, same wall: they don't have the actual words for the call, the email, the follow-up. Not theory. Not frameworks. The words.

The big companies solve this by hiring a CRO at $300K to $500K a year. But what about the founder closing deals themselves? The startup with three reps that can't afford a full-time CRO? The 200-agent BPO whose ops director is firefighting six campaigns at once? That is who we built this for - three of them, under one platform.
